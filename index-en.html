My approach

My experiences and projects have progressively taught me to value understanding the need, data quality, and evaluation as much as the choice of the model itself. This is the way of working I aim to deepen today.

1. Understanding the problem

Before modeling, I try to understand the context, the constraints, and how the results will actually be used.

2. Caring for the data

I pay close attention to their origin, consistency, and quality, because a large part of an analysis's reliability is decided at this stage.

3. Evaluating with perspective

I don't just look for a good metric. I also try to analyze the model's stability, its errors, and the trade-offs between performance and interpretability.

4. Moving towards something usable

Whenever possible, I try to extend the analysis into a tangible tool: an API, a dashboard, a scoring engine, or an evaluation protocol.
