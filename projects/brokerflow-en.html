BrokerFlow AI — controlled decisions

Keep decisions explainable when a model, rules and limited agentic parts coexist.

TypePrototype
Benchmark50 annotated cases
AUC0.728
Tests44

Architecture

  • Scoring model and deterministic rules kept separate.
  • Fallback and reason codes.
  • Constrained agents, not an uncontrolled autonomous system.

Evaluation

On 50 annotated cases: AUC 0.728, Brier 0.150, threshold 0.231 (values declared in the portfolio source of truth). Small sample: useful for a prototype, not for generalisation.

Limitations

  • Small benchmark.
  • No external production validation.
  • Separate model score from full-system behaviour.
