Case study · 2025

Evaluating RAG systems on long documents

At Square Management, I designed a protocol to compare RAG pipelines and understand where answers fail.

OrganizationSquare Management
RoleAI & Data Science intern
Year2025
Scope189 configurations

Question

How do OCR, chunking, retrieval and ranking choices affect answer quality on long documents?

Approach

  • Shared experimental plan across configurations.
  • Error analysis split across retrieval, ranking and generation.
  • Automated metrics, RAGAS-style signals and manual review of edge cases.
  • Vector search component (ChromaDB) exposed with FastAPI / Docker.

Publishable outcomes

  • 189 configurations compared.
  • Reusable evaluation framework.
  • Failure taxonomy: retrieval, ranking, generation.

Source documents, questions and detailed scores remain internal.

Limitations

  • Client data and deliverables are not public.
  • No external production metrics are published here.
